Core Web Vitals are how Google scores the experience of your pages: how fast content appears, how quickly the page responds, and how stable the layout feels. In Next.js, most of the wins come from a short checklist.

## The short checklist

- **Serve modern formats.** AVIF first, WebP as fallback. The `next/image` component does this for you when configured.
- **Size your images.** Always pass `sizes` so the browser downloads the right variant — never a 2400px hero for a 360px phone.
- **Prioritize the LCP image.** Only the hero gets `priority`. Everything below the fold stays lazy.
- **Stabilize layout.** Give images explicit dimensions or aspect ratios so content does not jump.

## Measure before you optimize

```bash
npx lighthouse https://your-site.com --only-categories=performance
```

Run it on mobile throttling, not your fiber-connected laptop. The field is where your users live.

## What good looks like

| Metric | Target |
| ------ | ------ |
| LCP | under 2.5s |
| INP | under 200ms |
| CLS | under 0.1 |

Hit those three on a mid-range phone and your site will feel instant to almost everyone.
